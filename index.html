Our Story

A Sanctuary of Elegance

Located along Roman Highway in Alas-Asin, Mariveles, Bataan, Dudice Traveller's Inn is your home away from home — offering clean, comfortable, and affordable accommodations with 24-hour service and a warm Filipino hospitality.

With air-conditioned rooms, free WiFi, private parking, and friendly staff, we make sure every guest enjoys a relaxing and hassle-free stay.
