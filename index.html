Before you arrive

A few useful answers.

Something else on your mind? Give us a call.

What time can I check in?

Arrival is flexible, subject to room availability. Your booked 12 or 24 hours start from your actual check-in time. Please tell us your expected arrival time so our team can confirm arrangements.

Are the displayed rates for reservations?

The room cards show walk-in rates for the included guests. Contact our team for your reservation rate, availability, and any group or long-stay arrangements.

How do I confirm a reservation?

Message us with your room choice, arrival details, stay length, and guest count. Our team will confirm availability and payment instructions. A booking is confirmed only after the required deposit is received and verified. See our booking terms.

Can I bring extra guests?

Each room lists the included and maximum number of guests. Extra guests cost ₱250 each. Confirm the charge period, sleeping arrangements, and final total with our team before booking.

What if I need to change or cancel my stay?

Contact us as soon as possible. Changes depend on availability, and cancellation charges may apply. Read the cancellation and refund policy before paying.

Is parking available?

We offer free parking. Let us know if you are bringing a vehicle, especially when arranging a group stay.

How can I check an existing reservation?

Reply in your existing Messenger conversation, or call +63 995 130 2152 with your booking name and arrival date. Our team can help confirm the status.
